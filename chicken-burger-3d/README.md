# The Ultimate Crispy Chicken Burger: full 3D animated cooking film

A 26.5-second, 1080 × 1920 (9:16), 30 fps fully CGI cooking commercial,
built entirely from Python inside Blender 5.2 (Cycles). There is no live
footage anywhere in it. Every ingredient, cooking action, particle, camera
move, sound effect and note of music is generated by the scripts in this
folder.

## The film, shot by shot

| # | Shot | Frames | What happens |
|---|------|--------|--------------|
| 1 | `S1_intro` | 75 | The finished burger floats and rotates among drifting crumbs and sesame seeds. The camera dollies in and pushes into the crispy chicken layer, over kinetic type: *CRISPY. CHEESY. STACKED.* |
| 2 | `S2_ingredients` | 120 | Chicken fillet, seasoning, egg wash, flour, buns, sauce, lettuce and cheese fly in with overshoot and spin, then settle into a floating grid with tracked 3D labels. The chicken rushes towards the camera into the next shot. |
| 3 | `S3_prep` | 150 | **01 SEASON**: a shaker rattles three bursts of spice that land and stick on the raw fillet (BVH-raycast landings). **02 DIP**: the fillet dips into egg wash with ripples, splash and drips. **03 COAT**: it drops into flour with a powder puff, flour rains down and sticks, and the crust displacement grows. It then flips, shakes off and gets a macro push-in with a "CRISPY COATING" callout. |
| 4 | `S4_fry` | 135 | **04 FRY**: tongs lower the coated fillet into a pot of oil, causing a splash, ring ripples and a foam ring of popping bubbles. Floating crispy bits, rising steam and heat haze follow. The chicken flips and browns progressively from pale to golden. Then it cuts to a macro close-up as the chicken is lifted out, dripping oil, with "GOLDEN BROWN" and "CRUNCHY CRUST" callouts. |
| 5 | `S5_build` | 120 | **05 BUILD**: the layers drop in with squash-and-stretch: bun, sauce (squeezed in a spiral from a bottle that follows the line), lettuce, cheese (which melts and drapes), crispy chicken (with a crumb burst) and the top bun. Each layer gets a label as it lands. |
| 6 | `S6_exploded` | 90 | Exploded view: every layer separates and floats while the camera orbits, with labels on each component. The layers then slam back together in sequence. |
| 7 | `S7_final` | 105 | Hero shot: the burger turns on its board with steam, floating crumbs and a light sweep. The extruded 3D title flips in: **THE ULTIMATE / CRISPY CHICKEN / BURGER**. |

Every shot length is a multiple of 15 frames, so all cuts land on a beat of the
120 BPM soundtrack.

## About the reference video

The uploaded cooking video **did not reach the build session**: no file was
attached and nothing was in the media library. The recipe and its order were
therefore reconstructed from the written brief:

- **Ingredients:** chicken, flour, seasoning, sauce, lettuce, cheese and burger buns.
- **Preparation:** "seasoned, dipped, coated", then fried.
- **Assembly:** in the order the brief lists it (bun, sauce, lettuce, cheese, crispy chicken, top bun).

An **egg wash** was added because the "dip" step needs a liquid.

Everything recipe-specific is in **`config.py`**, so the film can be matched to
the real footage by editing data, not code:

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| `INGREDIENTS` | Line-up order and on-screen labels (Scene 2) |
| `PREP_STEPS`, `FRY_TITLE`, `BUILD_TITLE` | Step numbers and titles |
| `ASSEMBLY_ORDER` | Bottom-to-top build order. It drives Scenes 1, 5, 6 and 7, including drop timing, label sides and the cheese drape |
| `LAYER_LABELS` | Exploded-view and build labels |
| `SAUCE_COLOR` | Sauce colour |
| `FRY_TEMPERATURE_LABEL` | Default `None`, so no temperature is shown. Set it, e.g. `"175°C"`, **only if verified** from the source video; an animated temperature badge then appears in Scene 4 |
| `FINAL_TEXT` | The end title |
| `SHOTS` | Shot lengths |

Then run `python build.py` and re-render.

## Pipeline

```
build.py   -> out/chicken_burger.blend   (7 shot scenes + FULL_FILM edit scene)
           -> out/timeline.json          (overlay events, 3D anchor tracks, sound cues)
render.py  -> out/frames/<shot>/####.png (Cycles, resumable)
audio.py   -> out/soundtrack.wav         (synthesised music + foley, cue-synced)
post.py    -> out/chicken_burger_3d.mp4  (upscale, FX, motion graphics, H.264 + AAC)
```

`./make_all.sh` runs everything. Useful options:

```bash
python build.py --only S4_fry                         # iterate on one shot
python render.py --shots S4_fry --range 80-90         # partial / resumed render
python render.py --shots S7_final --scale 0.25 --samples 6 --step 5   # quick preview
python post.py --stills 300,620                       # composite single frames to PNG
```

### Requirements
* Python 3.13 with `bpy==5.2.3` (`pip install bpy==5.2.3`), or Blender 5.2 itself
  (`blender -b -P build.py`)
* `numpy scipy pillow opencv-python-headless`
* `ffmpeg` (libx264 + aac)

### Opening the project in Blender
Open `out/chicken_burger.blend`. Each shot is its own scene (`S1_intro` … `S7_final`)
with its own camera, lights and world. The `FULL_FILM` scene strings them
together in the Video Sequencer for playback of the whole film. No baking is
required: every particle system is analytic (see below), so any frame can be
scrubbed or rendered on its own.

## How it is built (techniques)

* **Procedural food modelling** (`cb/models.py`):
  * Buns are revolved profiles. The top bun has 70 sesame seeds placed on the dome along its surface normals.
  * The chicken fillet is a deformed super-ellipsoid with organic lobes.
  * Lettuce is two ruffled radial leaves with noise-driven waves.
  * The cheese slice has a "drape" shape key that melts over the layer below.
  * The sauce is a bevelled spiral curve animated with `bevel_factor_end`.
  * The props (bowls, pot, shaker, squeeze bottle with a squeeze shape key, tongs and boards) are all generated from code too.
* **Crispy crust** (`cb/fx.py → FX_Crust`): a Geometry Nodes subdivision plus a domain-warped noise, ridge and Voronoi displacement. Its `Coat` input is keyframed, so the coating physically grows while the flour sticks. It also writes a `crag` attribute that the shader uses for browning.
* **One animated chicken material** (`cb/materials.py → chicken`) with four keyframed controls:
  * `season`: paprika and pepper flecks appear;
  * `wet`: egg-wash gloss;
  * `coat`: matte flour;
  * `fry`: a browning ramp from pale through golden to deep golden, with an oily sheen.
* **Analytic particles** (`FX_Ballistic`, `FX_Loop`): every particle's position is computed from the frame number inside Geometry Nodes, so no simulation cache is needed.
  * Landing times are pre-computed by ray-casting each trajectory against the chicken's BVH, so spice and flour really land and stick on its surface.
  * The same two node groups drive spice bursts, flour puffs, flour rain, egg drips, oil splashes, the bubble foam ring, crispy bits, steam sprites, crumb bursts and the floating crumbs and seeds.
* **Liquids**: a Geometry Nodes ripple group (animated 4D noise plus an expanding ring wave triggered at impact) on the egg wash and the oil.
* **Animation**: everything is keyed per frame from easing functions (back, elastic, ease in/out, damped-cosine impacts) with volume-preserving squash and stretch on every landing.
* **Look**:
  * Cycles with an OIDN denoise.
  * Khronos PBR Neutral view transform. AgX shifts saturated yellows and oranges such as cheese, egg and crust towards salmon.
  * A food-commercial light rig: a warm soft key, a cool fill, two rim strips and a top light.
  * A seamless dark studio backdrop with a warm glow, and shadow-catcher floors so there is no horizon line.
* **Post** (`post.py`):
  * Lanczos upscale, heat-haze distortion above the oil, bloom, grade, vignette and grain.
  * Whip and zoom-blur transitions with a light flash on every cut.
  * All 2D motion graphics are drawn at native 1080 × 1920 so text stays crisp: step headers, tracked ingredient and layer labels (anchored to projected 3D positions), callout arrows, kinetic type and sparkles.
* **Sound** (`audio.py`): fully synthesised.
  * **Foley:** sizzling oil with crackles, crunches, spice-shaker rattles, sauce squeeze, landing thuds, splashes, drips, tong clacks, flour puffs, whooshes, a riser and impacts.
  * **Music:** a 120 BPM groove with FM electric piano, bass, drums and pads, ducked under the sizzle.
  * **Sync:** every event is placed from the cue sheet that `build.py` exports, so the sound lands on the exact animation frame.

## Render settings used for the delivered video

The delivered film was rendered at 60 % (648 × 1152), 12 adaptive samples with
OIDN, on a 4-core CPU with no GPU, then upscaled to 1080 × 1920 in `post.py`,
where all text is drawn at full resolution. On a machine with a GPU, set
`RENDER_SCALE = 1.0` and `SAMPLES = 64` in `config.py` for a native-resolution
master. Nothing else needs to change.
