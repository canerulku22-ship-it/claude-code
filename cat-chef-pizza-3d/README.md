# Chef Cat's Pepperoni Pizza: full 3D animated cooking film

A 24-second, 1080 × 1920 (9:16), 30 fps fully CGI cooking film starring an
original cartoon chef: an orange tabby cat in a white toque and a red
neckerchief who makes the whole pizza.  It is built entirely from Python
inside Blender 5.2 (Cycles): every ingredient, flame, camera move, sound
effect, meow and note of music is generated by the scripts in this folder.

It is the cat version of `../pepperoni-pizza-3d` and shares its pizza,
oven, cheese-pull and topping effects, with the same smaller 2D graphics
(`UI_SCALE = 0.7`).

## The chef

`cb/cat.py` builds the cat from simple shapes:

* a pear-shaped body, a round head with fluffy cheeks, a cream muzzle,
  pink nose and toe beans, and tabby stripes from a wave texture;
* big amber eyes with round pupils and catchlights; the eyes aim at any
  target, blink, squint and wink;
* ears that twitch, a swishing tail, a mouth that opens, and a tongue for
  lip-licking;
* rubber-hose arms: each arm is a NURBS curve from the shoulder to a paw,
  re-solved every frame, so a paw can reach anything in the scene.

`Cat.animate(frames, pose)` keys every part from a per-frame pose function
(root, lean, head, look target, blink, mouth, ears and both paw positions),
so each shot describes the performance in plain Python.

## The film, shot by shot

| # | Shot | Frames | What the chef does |
|---|------|--------|--------------------|
| 1 | `S1_intro` | 60 | Sits behind the finished pizza, nods and squints to *CRISPY. CHEESY. LOADED.*, throws a ta-da, then waves and meows to *LET'S COOK*. |
| 2 | `S2_ingredients` | 105 | Peeks up from the bottom of the frame, eyes following each ingredient as it lands, and lunges at the pepperoni as it flies at the camera. |
| 3 | `S3_stretch` | 105 | **01 STRETCH**: rubs flour over the dough ball, kneads it flat with alternating paws ("making biscuits"), tosses it spinning into the air, sneezes at the flour puff, and pats the puffy edge. |
| 4 | `S4_top` | 135 | **02 SAUCE**: drops a dollop off a spoon and spreads it in a spiral. **03 TOP**: showers mozzarella from both paws, deals out 24 pepperoni slices one by one, rubs oregano over, then ta-da. |
| 5 | `S5_bake` | 105 | **04 BAKE**: seen from inside the wood-fired oven, the chef pushes the pizza in on the peel, pulls the peel out, then peeks over the oven sill, licking its lips, as the cheese bubbles and the crust browns. |
| 6 | `S6_slice` | 105 | **05 SLICE**: rolls the pizza wheel through four cuts, lifts a slice in a long cheese pull, then swings it round and takes a bite. |
| 7 | `S7_final` | 105 | Holds up its slice, sprinkles herbs, blows a chef's kiss with a wink, and the 3D title **CHEF CAT'S / PEPPERONI / PIZZA** slams in. |

## Sound

Everything is synthesised in `audio.py`, including the chef's voice:
`meow` (a voiced glide through moving formants), `purr` (noise pulsed at
about 25 Hz), a tiny `sneeze`, and a happy `nom` on the bite.  The rest is
the pizza film's foley and 120 BPM groove.

## Pipeline

```
build.py   -> out/cat_chef_pizza.blend (7 shot scenes + FULL_FILM edit scene)
           -> out/timeline.json        (overlay events, 3D anchor tracks, sound cues)
render.py  -> out/frames/<shot>/####.png
audio.py   -> out/soundtrack.wav
post.py    -> out/cat_chef_pizza_3d.mp4
```

`./make_all.sh` runs everything.  See `../pepperoni-pizza-3d/README.md` for
how the pizza, toppings, oven and cheese pull are built.

## Render settings used for the delivered video

Rendered at 60 % (648 × 1152) with 10 adaptive samples and OIDN on a 4-core
CPU, then upscaled to 1080 × 1920 in `post.py`, where all text is drawn at
full resolution.  For a native-resolution master set `RENDER_SCALE = 1.0`
and `SAMPLES = 64` in `config.py`.
