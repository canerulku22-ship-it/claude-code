# The Classic Pepperoni Pizza: full 3D animated cooking film

A 24-second, 1080 × 1920 (9:16), 30 fps fully CGI cooking commercial, built
entirely from Python inside Blender 5.2 (Cycles). There is no live footage in
it. Every ingredient, shred of cheese, slice of pepperoni, flame, camera move,
sound effect and note of music is generated by the scripts in this folder.

It is built with the same toolkit as `../spaghetti-bolognese-3d` and
`../chicken-spaghetti-3d`, with the same smaller 2D graphics
(`UI_SCALE = 0.7`) and food framed small in the frame.

## The film, shot by shot

| # | Shot | Frames | What happens |
|---|------|--------|--------------|
| 1 | `S1_intro` | 60 | A sliced pepperoni pizza turns on a wooden board, one slice pulled out. Kinetic type reads *CRISPY. CHEESY. LOADED.*, then *LET'S COOK*, and the camera dives into the pizza. |
| 2 | `S2_ingredients` | 105 | Nine ingredients fly into a 3 × 3 grid with tracked labels: pizza dough, olive oil, mozzarella, pepperoni, garlic, parmesan, oregano, crushed tomatoes and basil. |
| 3 | `S3_stretch` | 105 | **01 STRETCH**: flour is dusted over a dough ball on a floured board. The ball presses out into a disc, is tossed spinning into the air, and lands bigger with a puff of flour. Callout: *LEAVE A PUFFY EDGE*. |
| 4 | `S4_top` | 135 | **02 SAUCE**: a dollop of tomato sauce lands and is spread out in a spiral with the back of a spoon. **03 TOP**: shredded mozzarella rains down, 24 pepperoni slices drop into place one by one, and oregano is sprinkled over. |
| 5 | `S5_bake` | 105 | **04 BAKE**: the pizza slides on a steel peel into a wood-fired brick oven with live flames. The camera flies in through the oven mouth while the cheese melts and bubbles, the crust puffs and browns with leopard-spot char, and the pepperoni cup and crisp. Callout: *BUBBLING & GOLDEN*. |
| 6 | `S6_slice` | 105 | **05 SLICE**: a pizza wheel rolls through the pizza four times, opening up eight slices. One slice is lifted away in a long cheese pull. Callout: *THE CHEESE PULL*. |
| 7 | `S7_final` | 105 | Hero shot: basil drops onto the pizza and oregano and chilli flakes fall. Then the extruded 3D title flips in: **THE CLASSIC / PEPPERONI / PIZZA**. |

Every shot length is a multiple of 15 frames, so all cuts land on a beat of the
120 BPM soundtrack.

## The recipe

1. **Stretch:** stretch the dough ball out by hand, leaving a puffy edge.
2. **Sauce:** spread crushed-tomato sauce (with garlic, oregano and olive oil) over the base.
3. **Top:** cover with shredded mozzarella and pepperoni, then sprinkle with oregano.
4. **Bake:** bake in a very hot oven until the cheese bubbles and the crust is golden.
5. **Slice:** cut into eight slices and serve with basil.

No oven temperature is shown in the film. The ingredient line-up, step titles
and end title are all in `config.py`.

## Pipeline

```
build.py   -> out/pepperoni_pizza.blend (7 shot scenes + FULL_FILM edit scene)
           -> out/timeline.json         (overlay events, 3D anchor tracks, sound cues)
render.py  -> out/frames/<shot>/####.png (Cycles, resumable)
audio.py   -> out/soundtrack.wav        (synthesised music + foley, cue-synced)
post.py    -> out/pepperoni_pizza_3d.mp4 (upscale, FX, motion graphics, H.264 + AAC)
```

`./make_all.sh` runs everything. Useful options:

```bash
python build.py --only S6_slice                         # iterate on one shot
python render.py --shots S6_slice --range 50-90         # partial / resumed render
python render.py --shots S5_bake --scale 0.3 --samples 6 --step 10   # quick preview
python post.py --stills 120,450                         # composite single frames to PNG
```

## How it is built

* **Pizza base** (`MD.pizza_base`): a crust profile revolved around the
  axis, with noise in the rim height and width so the edge is irregular.
  * Two shape keys drive it: `ball` (the same vertices mapped onto a dough
    ball, used for the press-out) and `puff` (the rim rising in the oven).
  * A `rim` attribute weights the browning towards the crust.
  * The same function builds single wedges with cut faces showing an airy
    crumb, so the baked pizza can really be sliced.
* **Toppings** (`MD.pizza_layer`): sauce and melted cheese are irregular-edged
  discs (or wedges of one) with noise height.
  * Shredded mozzarella and pepperoni are instanced particles.
  * The shred prototype has a `melt` shape key that flattens and widens it.
  * The pepperoni prototype has a `cup` shape key that curls its edges up.
  * Each shot gets its own copy of the prototype (`Shot.proto`), with its own
    animated material and shape keys.
* **Materials** (`cb/materials.py`):
  * Dough has `bake` and `flour` controls. Baking goes from raw, pale dough
    to a golden crust, then a browned rim with leopard-spot char.
  * Mozzarella has `melt` and `brown` controls: matte white shreds, then
    glossy molten cheese with golden blisters and orange pepperoni grease.
  * Pepperoni has a `bake` control: raw red with fat specks, then darker
    and glossy, with charred cupped edges.
  * Pizza sauce, floured wood, firebrick (mapped around the dome), oven
    stone, glowing embers and camera-only emissive flames.
* **Oven**: a brick dome with a front wall whose arched mouth is a boolean
  cut, logs with glowing cracks, and seven flame tongues.
  * The flames flicker through Geometry Nodes (`FX_Flame`) and time-varying
    shader noise.
  * Point lights with keyed flicker do the actual lighting, so the flames
    add no noise.
* **Cheese pull** (`FX_CheesePull`): 64 strings run between the pizza and
  the lifted slice.
  * Each string reads the slice's live transform (Object Info, relative)
    for its moving end.
  * The strings sag and thin as they stretch, then snap from the middle
    out at random lengths.
* **Pizza wheel**: rolls along four diameters, its wheel turning by the
  distance travelled. Each pass nudges the slices on either side of the cut
  apart, so the cut lines open up.
* **Particles** (`FX_Ballistic`, `FX_Loop`):
  * flour, mozzarella shreds, pepperoni, oregano and chilli flakes;
  * cheese bubbles, steam and flour puffs.
  * Falling toppings are solved to land flat (`final_rot`).
* **Sound** (`audio.py`): fully synthesised. Flour puffs, a dough slap, sauce
  splats, a crackling wood fire, bubbling cheese, the wheel crunching
  through the crust, whooshes and impacts, plus a 120 BPM groove with a
  build-up under the cheese pull.

## Render settings used for the delivered video

The film was rendered at 60 % (648 × 1152) with 10 adaptive samples and
OIDN, on a 4-core CPU with no GPU, then upscaled to 1080 × 1920 in `post.py`,
where all text is drawn at full resolution. On a machine with a GPU, set
`RENDER_SCALE = 1.0` and `SAMPLES = 64` in `config.py` for a
native-resolution master.
