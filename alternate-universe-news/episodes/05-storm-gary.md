# EP 05 — STORM GARY

> Port Oddly's most frequent eyewitness becomes a weather event. Afterwards, something from somewhere else answers.

**Runtime:** ~82s · **Tamsin's mic:** corn on the cob (it pops into popcorn) · **Mayor:** paragliding · **Clock:** AH… AH… · **Desk mirror:** real Desmond holding a "HELP" sign · **Desmond's parting:** RIGHT

## 1. Video headline
**BREAKING: LOCAL MAN'S SNEEZE OFFICIALLY NAMED A STORM — 40,000 EVACUATED**

## 2. First 3-second hook
- **Visual:** A weather map. A huge red storm spiral is centred on a cut-out of Gary's face, frozen mid-"ah".
- **VO:** "Gary Pembleton is about to sneeze. Forty thousand people have been evacuated."
- **Text:** RED WARNING: ONE SNEEZE

## 3. Voiceover script

**DESMOND:** Gary Pembleton is about to sneeze. Forty thousand people have been evacuated.

**DESMOND:** For six hours, Port Oddly's most frequent eyewitness has been stuck in the "ah… ah…" stage of what forecasters are calling the most dangerous sneeze in recorded history.

**QUIST:** I've studied this my entire life. Since he said "ah." The pressure in Mr. Pembleton's nose is higher than inside a hurricane. If he goes, we lose the coast. Possibly Belgium.

**TAMSIN** *(live)*: Desmond, I'm three metres from the nostrils. The air is thick with tension… and pollen.

**GARY** *(head tilted back, eyes watering)*: Ah… I just… ah… came out for… ah… milk.

**DESMOND:** Emergency crews have tried everything: staring at the sun, pinching the nose, and shouting "pineapple," which a woman on the internet swore by.

**MAYOR** *(video call, wind howling)*: Everyone must stay indoors! Like me! At my desk!

**DESMOND:** At 6:42pm… it happened.

**GARY:** AAAAH-CHOOOO!

**DESMOND:** Storm Gary made landfall at four hundred miles an hour. It stripped every roof on the high street, flattened a cornfield into the shape of a tissue, and was felt by a lighthouse in Iceland.

**DESMOND:** Then, silence. Until seismographs picked up a faint reply… from outside this universe. Experts have decoded it. It said: "Bless you."

**GARY** *(quietly)*: …Mum?

**DESMOND:** Mr. Pembleton has declined to comment.

**DESMOND:** Meanwhile, the blast hit Port Oddly Mall, where the escalator has now been going up for six hours. It has not stopped.

**DESMOND:** I'm Desmond Vale. Do not adjust your reality.

**S5 · 0:27–0:31 · Gary close-up**
Tight close-up of [GARY]'s face, trembling on the edge of the sneeze, one tear rolling down his cheek.

**S6 · 0:31–0:38 · Attempts**

[MAYOR] video call while paragliding over a sunny beach, fake office background flapping in the wind. A seagull smacks into the camera.

**S8 · 0:42–0:50 · The sneeze**
Slow motion: Gary's face contorts, then THE SNEEZE. A visible shockwave ripples outward across the town; roof tiles, hats and a postbox fly away. Tamsin's corn on the cob instantly explodes into popcorn in her hand. She keeps reporting.

**S9 · 0:50–0:58 · Aftermath**
Aerial shot of a cornfield flattened into the perfect shape of a pulled tissue. Cut to a remote Icelandic lighthouse on black rocks, wobbling slightly.

**S10 · 0:58–1:06 · The signal**
A dim laboratory: a seismograph needle scratches a faint, rhythmic signal. A monitor beside it decodes the signal into two words: "BLESS YOU".

**S11 · 1:06–1:10 · "…Mum?"**
Close-up of [GARY], nose red, eyes glistening, looking up at the evening sky, whispering.

**S12 · 1:10–1:22 · Sign-off**
[AUN STUDIO], [DESMOND] with hair parted on the RIGHT. On the desk, a small hand mirror shows the real Desmond holding a handwritten "HELP" sign. The LED wall shows a mall escalator stretching up into the clouds.
