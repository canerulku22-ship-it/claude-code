## 3. Voiceover script

**CHECKOUT** *(robotic)*: Unexpected item in the bagging area.

**DESMOND:** The unexpected item… is Port Oddly.

**DESMOND:** At 9am, a self-checkout at the FreshWay supermarket detected an unexpected item. Then another. By ten, it had scanned the entire town and sealed it inside a giant carrier bag.

**TAMSIN** *(live)*: Desmond, I'm inside the bag. It's warm. It's crinkly. And every few minutes, a voice asks us to please place our item in the bagging area. We are the item.

**DESMOND:** The machine has flagged the Mayor as an age-restricted product.

**MAYOR** *(video call)*: I am over eighteen, and I'm at my desk. *(ski lift creaks)*

**CHECKOUT:** Approval needed.

**DESMOND:** It has also asked Deputy Mayor Reginald for ID.

**MAUREEN:** The Deputy Mayor says he is a goose, and that should be enough.

**QUIST:** I've studied this my entire life. Since 2014, when I tried to buy one banana. These machines are never satisfied. I once placed a single grape in the bagging area. It called the police.

**DESMOND:** Officials pressed "call for assistance." The light flashed for six hours. Then an assistant arrived: Barry, seventy-four, who says he's been waiting for that light since 2009.

**DESMOND:** Barry walked over, tapped something on the screen, and said:

**BARRY:** There you go.

**DESMOND:** …and walked off. The bag opened. The town was free. To this day, nobody knows what he pressed.

**DESMOND:** But as the town was released, the machine printed one final receipt. Item four million and one: Gary Pembleton. Origin… Universe Zero.

**DESMOND:** Tomorrow: the truth about Gary.

**DESMOND:** I'm Desmond Vale. Do not adjust your reality.
