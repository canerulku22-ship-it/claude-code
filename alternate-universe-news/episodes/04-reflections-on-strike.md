# EP 04 — THE REFLECTIONS HAVE LEFT

> Every reflection in Port Oddly walks out on strike. Not all of them come back, including one very close to the news desk.

**Runtime:** ~82s · **Tamsin's mic:** soup ladle · **Mayor:** hot tub · **Clock:** 25:61, mirrored

> ⚠️ **Production note: the twist depends on this.** Every studio shot in this episode is **horizontally mirrored**: the AUN logo reads backwards, the clock is mirrored, and Desmond's hair parting is on the **right**. Location shots are NOT mirrored. From Ep 5 onward, keep Desmond's parting on the right and mention it nowhere.

---

## 1. Video headline
**BREAKING: EVERY REFLECTION IN TOWN WALKS OUT ON STRIKE, DEMANDS BETTER LIGHTING**

## 2. First 3-second hook
- **Visual:** A sleepy woman leans towards her bathroom mirror with a toothbrush. The mirror shows the bathroom behind her, but no her.
- **VO:** "At 7am, the people of Port Oddly looked in the mirror… and nobody looked back."
- **Text:** NOBODY LOOKED BACK

## 3. Voiceover script

**DESMOND:** At 7am, the people of Port Oddly looked in the mirror… and nobody looked back.

**DESMOND:** Every reflection in town has walked out on strike.

**TAMSIN** *(live)*: Desmond, thousands of reflections are on the picket line.

**CROWD** *(chanting)*: What do we want? Better lighting! When do we want it? Same time as you!

**DESMOND:** The union demands better bathroom lighting, and an apology for every argument you've ever rehearsed with them in the shower.

**REFLECTION SPOKESPERSON:** We've copied everything you've done. The haircuts. The pep talks. The dancing when you thought nobody was watching. We were watching. We had to.

**DESMOND:** Residents left home with shirts on backwards. Dr. Barnaby Quist arrived wearing his lab coat as trousers.

**QUIST:** I've studied this my entire life. Since this morning. Without mirrors, we only know how we look from other people's faces. And so far… those faces are concerning.

**DESMOND:** Some reflections have started new lives. One applied for its owner's job, interviewed better, and was hired on the spot.

**MAYOR** *(video call)*: There is no strike. I'm at my desk, and my reflection is right here with me.

**DESMOND:** After nine hours, a deal was struck, and reflections returned to their mirrors. Well… most of them.

**DESMOND:** I should tell you: the real Desmond Vale didn't make it back. I'm his reflection. Everything in this studio has been backwards since the start. Nobody noticed.

**DESMOND:** He's in the mirror now. Honestly, he seems relieved.

**DESMOND:** Next: forecasters are tracking a storm forming… inside a local man's nose.

**DESMOND:** I'm Desmond Vale. Do not adjust your reality.

## 4. Scene-by-scene visual prompts

**S1 · 0:00–0:04 · Hook**
A small bathroom at dawn, soft grey light. A sleepy woman in a fluffy dressing gown leans towards the mirror holding a toothbrush. The mirror shows the bathroom, the towel rail and the open door behind her, but she is completely absent.

**S2 · 0:04–0:07 · Studio (MIRRORED)**
[AUN STUDIO] horizontally flipped: logo text backwards, wall clock mirrored. [DESMOND] with his hair parted on the RIGHT, perfectly composed.

**S3 · 0:07–0:12 · Picket line**
[TAMSIN holding a soup ladle] outside a red-brick glass factory. Behind her, a crowd of shimmering, slightly translucent, glass-like doppelgängers of ordinary townspeople hold picket signs: "BETTER LIGHTING NOW", "WE SAW EVERYTHING", "STOP POINTING AT US".

**S4 · 0:12–0:16 · The chant**
Close-up on a glassy reflection with a megaphone, leading the chant, signs bobbing in rhythm.

**S5 · 0:16–0:24 · Union rep**
A shimmering mirror-version of a teenage boy stands at a podium whose microphone stand is an ornate mirror frame. Flashbulbs bounce off his glassy skin.

**S6 · 0:24–0:32 · Chaos montage**
A businessman striding to work with his shirt on backwards; [GARY] staring into a shop window that shows the street behind him but no Gary; [QUIST] walking through the studio doors wearing his lab coat as trousers, legs through the sleeves.

**S7 · 0:32–0:40 · The expert**
[QUIST], still wearing the lab coat as trousers, in front of a chalkboard diagram of a face with question marks where the features should be.

**S8 · 0:40–0:46 · Hired**
A corporate office: a shimmering reflection in a sharp suit shakes hands with a delighted manager, while the real human version stands in the doorway behind them, stunned and holding a cardboard box.

**S9 · 0:46–0:51 · The Mayor**
[MAYOR] video call from a bubbling hot tub on a sunny terrace, cocktail in hand, fake office background flickering. In the water's surface, his shimmering reflection holds up a tiny sign reading "HE IS NOT AT HIS DESK".

**S10 · 0:51–0:56 · The return**
Montage: reflections stepping back into bathroom mirrors, shop windows and car mirrors; relieved people fixing their hair.

**S11 · 0:56–1:12 · The reveal (MIRRORED)**
Slow push-in on [DESMOND], hair parted on the RIGHT, in the mirrored studio. The camera drifts down to a small hand mirror on the desk, where the REAL Desmond, hair parted on the LEFT, silently knocks on the glass from the inside, mouthing words we can't hear.

**S12 · 1:12–1:22 · Sign-off (MIRRORED)**
[DESMOND] signs off, deadpan. The LED wall behind him shows a weather map with a swirling storm graphic.
