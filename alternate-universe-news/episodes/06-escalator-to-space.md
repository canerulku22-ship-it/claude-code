## 3. Voiceover script

**DESMOND:** This escalator has been going up for three days. It is now taller than Mount Everest.

**DESMOND:** Since Storm Gary hit Port Oddly Mall, the escalator between Shoes and the food court has refused to stop, building new steps as it climbs.

**TAMSIN** *(live)*: Desmond, I'm on step forty-one thousand. We've passed the clouds and a very confused eagle. One man tried walking up on the left. He's two days ahead of us. We don't talk about him.

**DESMOND:** A society has formed. Step nine thousand now has a bakery, a school, and an elected council. Step nine thousand and one has declared independence.

**BRIDE:** We met on step three hundred. Got married on step twelve thousand. Honestly? It's all moving very fast.

**QUIST:** I've studied this my entire life. Since Wednesday. Sorry, since Gary. The escalator is powered by humanity's refusal to take the stairs. It's limitless.

**MAYOR** *(video call)*: I have personally ordered it to stop. From my desk. *(limbo music)*

**DESMOND:** This morning, it passed a space station.

**ASTRONAUT:** Four hundred people have asked us where the toilets are.

**DESMOND:** Finally, at 4:17 this afternoon, after seventy-two hours, the riders reached the top. Waiting for them… was a small yellow sign.

**DESMOND:** "Escalator out of order. Please use the stairs."

**DESMOND:** Riders are now walking down. Estimated arrival: March.

**DESMOND:** Meanwhile, Gary Pembleton, on step six, reports his left sock is missing. He's not alone. Four billion socks are unaccounted for… and they've all been traced to one dryer.

**DESMOND:** I'm Desmond Vale. Do not adjust your reality.
