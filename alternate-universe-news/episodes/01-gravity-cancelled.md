# EP 01 — GRAVITY CANCELLED

> Port Oddly forgets to renew its gravity subscription. A goose saves the day, and the town pays for it.

**Runtime:** ~80s · **Tamsin's mic:** AUN microphone · **Mayor:** jet ski · **Clock:** 25:61

## 3. Voiceover script

**DESMOND:** Gravity has been cancelled. Not broken. Cancelled. Someone forgot to pay.

**DESMOND:** At 9:14 this morning, Port Oddly received an email from its gravity provider, titled "Sorry to see you go." By 9:15, everything had gone.

**TAMSIN** *(live, floating)*: Desmond, I'm four hundred feet above the high street. Below me: the post office, several cows, and the under-twelves football team, who are somehow still winning.

**GARY** *(drifting past, upside down)*: I just came out for milk.

**MAYOR** *(video call)*: I want to be very clear: I am at my desk. *(jet ski engine roars)* That's the printer.

**QUIST:** I've studied this my entire life. Since about nine-fifteen. Gravity used to come free with the planet. Now there's Basic, Plus and Premium. Premium's the one where your car stays where you parked it.

**DESMOND:** The town tried to pay, but its card was declined for suspicious activity: namely, trying to stay on the ground.

**DESMOND:** By noon, one-hundred-and-three-year-old Edna Pocket had reached thirty thousand feet, where an airline offered her a window seat.

**EDNA** *(by phone, wind howling)*: Best day of my life. Don't come and get me.

**DESMOND:** At 4pm, the balance was paid in full, in bread, by a local goose named Reginald. Gravity is back. However, the town is on the free plan, so every fifteen minutes—

*(Everything in the studio floats. Cheesy jingle.)*

**AD VOICE:** This gravity is brought to you by Gravity Plus. Stay grounded, for just twelve ninety-nine a month.

*(Everything crashes down.)*

**DESMOND** *(papers on his head, unbothered)*: —there's a short advert.

**DESMOND:** Tonight, the council appointed Reginald as Deputy Mayor. His first policy is announced tomorrow. Sources say it involves… Wednesday.

**DESMOND:** I'm Desmond Vale. Do not adjust your reality.
