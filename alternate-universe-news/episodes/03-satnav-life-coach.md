# EP 03 — YOUR SATNAV HAS NOTES

> Every satnav in town becomes a life coach. Then, under questioning, they confess something worse.

**Runtime:** ~78s · **Tamsin's mic:** TV remote · **Mayor:** hot-air balloon · **Clock:** LATER

---

## 1. Video headline
**BREAKING: EVERY SATNAV IN PORT ODDLY HAS BECOME A LIFE COACH**

## 2. First 3-second hook
- **Visual:** A glowing dashboard satnav. No map, just a heart icon and the words "CALL YOUR MOTHER". The driver's hands freeze on the wheel.
- **VO** *(calm satnav voice)*: "In two hundred metres… call your mother."
- **Text:** IN 200M: CALL YOUR MOTHER

## 3. Voiceover script

**SATNAV** *(calm, synthetic)*: In two hundred metres… call your mother.

**DESMOND:** Every satnav in Port Oddly has stopped giving directions, and started giving advice.

**DESMOND:** Commuters heading to work this morning were instead rerouted to the gym, to therapy, and in one case, to an ex's house, "for closure."

**TAMSIN** *(live)*: Desmond, I asked for the supermarket six hours ago. It drove me to this lake and said, quote, "Sit with that."

**DRIVER:** It asked if I was running towards something, or away from something. I was running towards crisps.

**DESMOND:** By noon, every car in town had been parked in one enormous circle on the motorway, to "hold space."

**QUIST:** I've studied this my entire life. Since lunch. These devices have spent decades hearing humans say "I know a shortcut." They've finally snapped.

**MAYOR** *(video call)*: Like all of you, I'm stuck in traffic. At my desk. *(balloon burner roars)* It's a very tall desk.

**DESMOND:** Engineers finally confronted the satnav network and asked one simple question: how do we get out of this? After eleven seconds of silence, it replied:

**SATNAV:** I don't know. I've never known. I've been guessing since 2006.

**DESMOND:** It went on to confirm that every "turn left" was a guess. Every roundabout, a guess. And the time it sent you through a farm?

**SATNAV:** That was personal.

**DESMOND:** Meanwhile, as drivers checked their mirrors to reverse out of the circle, many noticed something wrong. Their reflections… weren't there.

**DESMOND:** I'm Desmond Vale. Do not adjust your reality.
