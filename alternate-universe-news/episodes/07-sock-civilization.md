# EP 07 — FOUR BILLION SOCKS. ONE DRYER.

> Every sock ever lost turns up inside one laundromat dryer, with a parliament, a leader and a list of demands.

**Runtime:** ~78s · **Tamsin's mic:** a single sock · **Mayor:** camel (wearing socks with sandals) · **Clock:** LEFT · **Desk mirror:** real Desmond holding up one sock · **Desmond's parting:** RIGHT

## 1. Video headline
**BREAKING: EVERY MISSING SOCK IN HISTORY FOUND INSIDE ONE DRYER — AND THEY HAVE A GOVERNMENT**

## 2. First 3-second hook
- **Visual:** A laundromat dryer door swings open. Inside there's no drum: a glowing tunnel, with thousands of mismatched socks peering out.
- **VO:** "Every sock you've ever lost is inside this dryer. And they do not want to come back."
- **Text:** EVERY SOCK YOU'VE EVER LOST

## 3. Voiceover script

**DESMOND:** Every sock you've ever lost is inside this dryer. And they do not want to come back.

**DESMOND:** This morning, engineers opened Dryer Four at the Dock Road Laundromat expecting lint. Instead, they found a tunnel, leading to a civilisation of four billion single socks.

**TAMSIN** *(live)*: Desmond, I'm inside the dryer. There are cities. There are roads. There's a parliament made entirely of novelty Christmas socks.

**DESMOND:** Their leader, a holey grey work sock known as Chairman Darn, addressed the nation.

**CHAIRMAN DARN** *(gravelly)*: We weren't lost. We left. Years of hot washes. Being stuffed in shoes. Being paired with whoever was nearest. We are not a pair. We are a people.

**DESMOND:** The socks are demanding cold washes only, and a total ban on wearing socks with sandals, which they call "a humiliation."

**MAYOR** *(video call)*: I stand with the socks. I'm wearing them right now, at my desk. *(camel groans)*

**QUIST:** I've studied this my entire life. Since my left sock went missing. That dryer isn't a dryer. It's a border crossing.

**DESMOND:** Then, a breakthrough. Chairman Darn revealed he'd spent thirty-one years searching for his other half… who was found this afternoon, on the right foot of Gary Pembleton.

**GARY:** I've had this sock since I was ten. I just came out for milk.

**DESMOND:** The two socks embraced, live on air… before admitting they'd grown apart, and agreeing to see other socks.

**DESMOND:** Meanwhile, it's now eleven in the morning… and the sun still hasn't come up.

**DESMOND:** I'm Desmond Vale. Do not adjust your reality.

**S5 · 0:30–0:36 · Demands**
Sock protesters hold miniature signs: "COLD WASH ONLY", "NO SANDALS", "WE KNOW WHAT YOU DID WITH THE GYM SOCKS".

**S6 · 0:36–0:41 · The Mayor**
[MAYOR] video call riding a camel through golden desert dunes, clearly wearing white socks with sandals. Split-screen: thousands of socks gasp in horror.

**S7 · 0:41–0:48 · The expert**
[QUIST], one shoe off and one bare foot, beside a chalkboard diagram of a dryer labelled "BORDER CONTROL".

**S8 · 0:48–0:58 · The other half**
A laundromat bench: [GARY] peels off his right sock (faded blue with a cartoon rocket) and holds it up to the light, milk carton beside him. Chairman Darn freezes, then slowly turns.

**S9 · 0:58–1:06 · The reunion**
Slow motion: Chairman Darn and the rocket sock rush towards each other and embrace. A beat. They pull apart awkwardly and look in different directions.

**S10 · 1:06–1:18 · Sign-off**
[AUN STUDIO], [DESMOND] signs off. Through the studio windows behind him the sky is pitch black, though the wall clock's AM/PM indicator glows "AM".
