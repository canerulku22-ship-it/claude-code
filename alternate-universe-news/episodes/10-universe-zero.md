# EP 10 — UNIVERSE ZERO (SEASON FINALE)

> Port Oddly's scientists discover the strangest universe yet, where nobody's mayor is a goose. Gary finally recognises home. And the anchor looks straight at you.

**Runtime:** ~85s · **Tamsin's mic:** brand-new microphone, still sealed in its plastic packaging · **Mayor:** actually at his desk · **Clock:** 2:00 AM · **Desk mirror:** real Desmond holding a "FOLLOW FOR SEASON 2" sign · **Desmond's parting:** RIGHT

## 2. First 3-second hook
- **Visual:** Desmond leans towards the lens and points straight at the viewer. Behind him, a swirling portal shows a dark bedroom lit by a phone screen.
- **VO:** "Scientists have discovered another universe. It's the strangest one ever found. And you're watching from it."
- **Text:** ANOTHER UNIVERSE FOUND. YOU LIVE THERE.

## 3. Voiceover script

**DESMOND:** Scientists have discovered another universe. It's the strangest one ever found. And you're watching from it.

**DESMOND:** After yesterday's receipt, Dr. Barnaby Quist has traced Gary Pembleton's origin to a place he calls Universe Zero.

**QUIST:** I've studied this my entire life. Since yesterday. It's disturbing. Their lifts have a "close door" button that does nothing, and they press it anyway. Their socks go missing, and nobody investigates. And at 2am, instead of sleeping, they lie in bed watching sixty-second videos… and call it relaxing.

**TAMSIN** *(live)*: Desmond, the town is in shock. One woman fainted when she learned that in Universe Zero… none of the mayors are geese.

**MAUREEN:** The Deputy Mayor… has no words.

**DESMOND:** Mayor Fennimore joined us from his desk.

**MAYOR** *(video call, quiet office)*: I'm at my desk.

**DESMOND:** For the first time, he actually is. Nobody believes him.

**DESMOND:** Then Gary Pembleton, witness to every incident this season, stepped forward.

**GARY:** I recognise it. It's home. Eleven years ago, I popped out for milk, took a wrong turn at a laundromat dryer, and ended up here. That "bless you," after the storm… that was my mum.

**DESMOND:** He has been trying to get home with the milk ever since. The milk has expired.

**DESMOND:** Next season: Gary goes home. But the way back only opens on a Wednesday… and we don't have those anymore.

**DESMOND:** One last thing. Our scientists can now see into Universe Zero. We can see you. It's late. You're in bed. You've watched this twice. Go to sleep.

**DESMOND:** I'm Desmond Vale. Do not adjust your reality.
