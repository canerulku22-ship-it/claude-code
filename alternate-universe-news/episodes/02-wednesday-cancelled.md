# EP 02 — WEDNESDAY CANCELLED

> The new goose Deputy Mayor bites Wednesday off the calendar. The replacement day gets a name nobody remembers voting for.

**Runtime:** ~80s · **Tamsin's mic:** hairbrush · **Mayor:** rollercoaster · **Clock:** GARY O'CLOCK

---

## 1. Video headline
**BREAKING: GOOSE DEPUTY MAYOR CANCELS WEDNESDAY — WEEK NOW HAS SIX DAYS**

## 2. First 3-second hook
- **Visual:** Extreme close-up: a goose in a tiny mayoral chain bites the WEDNESDAY column clean off a wall calendar, then stares into the lens, chewing.
- **VO:** "As of midnight, Wednesday no longer exists. And the goose responsible is not sorry."
- **Text:** WEDNESDAY HAS BEEN CANCELLED

## 3. Voiceover script

**DESMOND:** As of midnight, Wednesday no longer exists. And the goose responsible is not sorry.

**DESMOND:** In his first act as Deputy Mayor, Reginald walked onto the official town calendar and bit Wednesday clean off.

**REGINALD:** *(HONK.)*

**MAUREEN** *(interpreting)*: The Deputy Mayor says Wednesday has been mid for centuries, was never truly the middle of anything, and he will be taking no questions.

**TAMSIN** *(live)*: Desmond, residents went to bed on Tuesday and woke up directly in Thursday. It's like missing a stair, but for your whole week.

**DESMOND:** Office workers celebrated, until they remembered Wednesday was payday.

**QUIST:** I've studied this my entire life. Since breakfast. Without Wednesday, Tuesday and Thursday are now touching. And they're… getting close.

**DESMOND:** Wednesday itself was later spotted in a neighbouring town, where it's thriving, has started a podcast, and says it finally "feels seen."

**MAYOR** *(video call)*: I fully support the Deputy Mayor. I'm at my desk right now— *(rollercoaster drops; screaming)* —VERY BUSY DESK!

**DESMOND:** After public outcry, the council held an emergency vote to name a replacement day. In a unanimous result, the new day is called… Gary.

**GARY:** I don't know how I feel about it. I just came out for milk.

**DESMOND:** Gary Pembleton, who also witnessed yesterday's gravity incident, says he never entered the vote. Officials say nobody remembers writing his name. And yet, every single ballot… said Gary.

**DESMOND:** Meanwhile, drivers across town report their satnavs have started giving… life advice.

**DESMOND:** I'm Desmond Vale. Do not adjust your reality.

**S7 · 0:41–0:47 · The Mayor**
[MAYOR] video call from the front car of a rollercoaster cresting the top of a huge drop, clutching a "WORLD'S BEST MAYOR" coffee mug, fake office background glitching.

**S8 · 0:47–0:55 · The new week**
A freshly printed town calendar being unrolled on the council table: "MON · TUE · GARY · THU · FRI · SAT · SUN". Officials applaud politely.

**S9 · 0:55–1:00 · Gary**
[GARY] standing beneath the new calendar outside the town hall, holding his milk, blinking slowly.

**S10 · 1:00–1:10 · The ballots**
A dim election hall. Officials tip out ballot boxes and every single ballot paper reads "GARY", each in different handwriting. Slow, eerie push-in on the pile.

**S11 · 1:10–1:20 · Sign-off**
[AUN STUDIO], [DESMOND] signs off. Through the window behind him, a parked car's dashboard glows with a satnav screen reading "ARE YOU HAPPY?"
