# EP 08 — THE SUN PRESSED SNOOZE

> One man hits snooze 412 times, and the sun waits for him every time. When he finally wakes, he checks the date.

**Runtime:** ~82s · **Tamsin's mic:** electric toothbrush (switched on, buzzing) · **Mayor:** nightclub · **Clock:** SNOOZE · **Desk mirror:** real Desmond asleep in an eye mask · **Desmond's parting:** RIGHT

---

## 1. Video headline
**BREAKING: LOCAL MAN HITS SNOOZE 412 TIMES. THE SUN HAS NOT RISEN IN THREE DAYS.**

## 2. First 3-second hook
- **Visual:** A dark bedroom, an alarm clock reading "SNOOZE ×412". A hand slaps the button, and outside the window the rising sun sinks back below the horizon.
- **VO:** "The sun has not risen in three days… because one man keeps pressing snooze."
- **Text:** THE SUN HAS NOT RISEN IN 3 DAYS

## 3. Voiceover script

**DESMOND:** The sun has not risen in three days… because one man keeps pressing snooze.

**DESMOND:** At 7am on Gary, local man Kevin Marsh's alarm went off. He pressed snooze. And for reasons nobody can explain… so did the sun.

**QUIST:** I've studied this my entire life. Since I woke up, which was hard. Every time Mr. Marsh hits snooze, the sun waits nine more minutes. He's done it four hundred and twelve times.

**TAMSIN** *(live, whispering)*: Desmond, I'm outside his bedroom window. It's pitch black, it's freezing, and I can confirm he is still asleep. And drooling.

**DESMOND:** The world has adapted. Roosters have filed for unemployment. Owls are working triple shifts. Vampires are calling it "the best week of our lives."

**MAYOR** *(video call, shouting over bass)*: These are dark times. I've been at my desk for seventy-two hours straight!

**DESMOND:** Officials tried an air horn, a marching band, and a phone call from his mother. Deputy Mayor Reginald was sent in personally.

**REGINALD:** *(HONK.)*

**MAUREEN:** The Deputy Mayor says: let him sleep. He's doing great.

**DESMOND:** Then, at 3am, a breakthrough. Two rooms away, someone quietly opened a packet of crisps.

**DESMOND:** Kevin Marsh woke. The sun shot into the sky. The planet cheered. Then Mr. Marsh checked his phone, saw that it was Saturday… and went back to sleep.

**DESMOND:** Sunrise is now expected Monday.

**DESMOND:** With most of the town awake, residents rushed to the supermarket… where the self-checkout has detected an unexpected item.

**DESMOND:** I'm Desmond Vale. Do not adjust your reality.

**S4 · 0:21–0:28 · Live report**
Night-time suburban garden. [TAMSIN holding a buzzing electric toothbrush], wearing a woolly hat over her red trench coat, crouched beneath a bedroom window. Inside, a man sleeps face-down, drooling onto his pillow. Behind her, a crowd holds torches, including [GARY] holding his milk.

**S5 · 0:28–0:36 · The world adapts**
Montage: a rooster in a tiny necktie queuing at an unemployment office; an exhausted owl at an office desk surrounded by three coffee cups; a pale, elegant vampire on a sun lounger under the night sky, wearing sunglasses, giving a thumbs-up.

**S6 · 0:36–0:41 · The Mayor**
[MAYOR] video call from a neon-lit nightclub, dancing on a podium waving glow sticks, sweat glistening, fake office background glitching in the strobe light.

**S7 · 0:41–0:52 · Wake-up attempts**
The cramped bedroom: a firefighter blasting an air horn, a full marching band squeezed against the wardrobe, a phone on speaker labelled "MUM", and [REGINALD] standing on the pillow beside the sleeping man's head. [MAUREEN] in her inset, flapping gently and serenely.

**S8 · 0:52–0:57 · The crisps**
A dark kitchen two rooms away: a hand slowly, carefully peels open a bag of crisps. Smash-cut to an extreme close-up of Kevin's eyes snapping open.

**S9 · 0:57–1:02 · Sunrise**
The sun rockets into the sky in seconds, flooding the town with gold light. The crowd outside cheers, hugs and sets off fireworks. Tamsin wipes away a single tear.

**S10 · 1:02–1:10 · Saturday**
Kevin sits up in bed and squints at his phone lock screen: "SATURDAY 3:02". He flops back onto the pillow. Through the window behind him, the sun drops out of the sky like a stone.

**S11 · 1:10–1:22 · Sign-off**
[AUN STUDIO], [DESMOND] signs off. The LED wall behind him shows a supermarket self-checkout with a red light flashing on top.
