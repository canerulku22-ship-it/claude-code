# ALTERNATE UNIVERSE NEWS — Season 1

> **Logline:** A dead-serious news channel from a universe that is slightly, catastrophically wrong.
> Every bulletin is filed from **Port Oddly**, a small seaside town where reality is held together with bread, tape and denial.

Ten 70–90 second episodes. Each one stands alone, but every episode plants the next, and running gags pay off across the season. Viewers who binge get rewarded; viewers who pause get rewarded more.

## The cast

Each character has a **character lock**: a fixed description to paste wherever a scene prompt uses their `[TAG]`. Use the same lock (and, if your tool supports it, the same reference image or seed) every time so the cast stays consistent across all 10 videos.

### DESMOND VALE — Anchor `[DESMOND]`
> distinguished male news anchor in his late 50s, silver swept-back hair with a sharp side parting on the LEFT, strong jaw, pale grey eyes, completely expressionless, immaculate navy three-piece suit, burgundy tie, white pocket square, seated at a glossy black curved news desk

- **Voice:** deep, slow, velvet gravitas. Never laughs. Never reacts. Treats a goose with the same respect as a head of state.
- **Sign-off (every episode):** *"I'm Desmond Vale. Do not adjust your reality."*
- **Secret:** From Episode 4 onward, the anchor is Desmond's **reflection**. Flip the hair parting to the **RIGHT** from Ep 4 on. Nobody on screen ever mentions it.

### TAMSIN KROLL — Field Reporter `[TAMSIN]`
> determined female field reporter in her early 30s, sleek black chin-length bob, bright red trench coat, AUN press lanyard, windswept but immaculately composed, holding [MIC OBJECT] to her mouth exactly like a microphone, looking straight into camera

- **Voice:** brisk, breathless, totally committed.
- **Gag:** Budget cuts. Her microphone is a worse object every episode, and she never acknowledges it.

### MAYOR DOUG FENNIMORE `[MAYOR]`
> sunburned cheerful man in his early 60s, round face, aviator sunglasses pushed up on his forehead, gold mayoral chain worn over a loud Hawaiian shirt, shown inside a laggy video-call window with a glitching "office bookshelf" virtual background

- **Voice:** jovial, shouting over background noise.
- **Gag:** Joins every crisis by video call, obviously on holiday, insisting *"I am at my desk."* In the finale he really is at his desk, and nobody believes him.

### DEPUTY MAYOR REGINALD — a goose `[REGINALD]`
> large white domestic goose wearing a tiny gold mayoral chain and a small navy necktie, standing on a council podium covered in microphones, intense unblinking stare

- **Voice:** real goose honk SFX only.

### MAUREEN DOBBS — Official Goose Interpreter `[MAUREEN]`
> stern woman in her mid-50s, grey cardigan, pearl necklace, reading glasses on a chain, shown in a circular inset in the bottom-right corner of the frame like a sign-language interpreter, flapping her arms and craning her neck like a goose

- **Voice:** posh, eloquent, weirdly passionate. One honk becomes a whole paragraph.

### DR. BARNABY QUIST — Expert `[QUIST]`
> wild-haired scientist in his mid-40s, round tortoiseshell glasses, rumpled lab coat with too many pens in the pocket and a mustard stain, standing in front of a chalkboard covered in chaotic diagrams

- **Voice:** fast, nasal, delighted by disaster.
- **Gags:** Every interview opens with *"I've studied this my entire life. Since [very recently]."* His lower-third credential is different, and worse, every episode.

### GARY PEMBLETON, 41 — Witness (Again) `[GARY]`
> ordinary balding man in his early 40s, beige anorak zipped to the chin, holding a carton of milk, completely flat unbothered expression

- **Voice:** monotone.
- **Gag:** He is present at every incident and always says *"I just came out for milk."*
- **Season secret:** He did. Eleven years ago. In *our* universe.

## Season arc (spoilers)

1. **Ep 1:** A goose pays the town's gravity bill and becomes Deputy Mayor.
2. **Ep 2:** The goose cancels Wednesday. The replacement day is named "Gary" by a unanimous vote that nobody remembers casting.
3. **Ep 4:** The anchor is quietly replaced by his own reflection. The real Desmond is now stuck in the little mirror on the desk.
4. **Ep 5:** After Gary's sneeze, a "Bless you" arrives from *outside this universe*. Gary whispers: "…Mum?"
5. **Ep 7:** The laundromat dryer turns out to be a doorway.
6. **Ep 9:** A self-checkout receipt lists Gary's origin as **Universe Zero**.
7. **Ep 10:** Universe Zero is *ours*. Gary popped out for milk eleven years ago and took a wrong turn at a laundromat dryer. The way home only opens on a **Wednesday**, which the goose cancelled in Ep 2. That's the Season 2 hook.

## Production kit

### Global style suffix
Append this to every visual prompt:
> photorealistic, shot like a high-budget TV news broadcast, vertical 9:16 framing, cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, crisp 4K detail, deadpan serious tone

**Text in images:** AI generators garble long text. Add lower thirds, banners and the ticker in the edit. Only generate text inside the image when the prop *is* the joke (a sign, a screen, a receipt), and keep it to six words or fewer.

### Studio master prompt `[AUN STUDIO]`
> a sleek modern TV news studio in deep navy and crimson, a huge curved LED wall showing a spinning globe logo where one continent is shaped like a duck, a glossy black curved news desk, a large wall clock, tall windows behind the desk looking out over a small seaside town

### Brand pack
- **Logo:** the AUN globe with the duck-shaped continent.
- **Lower thirds:** crimson bar, white all-caps name, small navy tag (LIVE / WITNESS / EXPERT / "AT HIS DESK").
- **Breaking sting:** a 2-second, 3-note orchestral hit with a digital whoosh, identical every episode. The outro sting is the same three notes, slower.
- **Music bed:** tense, pulsing synth-orchestral "cable news" loop at about 120 BPM, ducked under the voiceover.

### Rules for every episode
1. **Hook inside 2 seconds.** Open mid-crisis. Never "Hello and welcome."
2. **Burn in word-by-word subtitles** for all voiceover, because most people watch muted. The captions in each script are the *designed graphics* on top: banners, lower thirds and the ticker.
3. **Italic text in the scripts is direction.** Strip it before sending lines to text-to-speech.
4. **Use original or royalty-free SFX.** Don't use real emergency-alert tones: platforms may mute the video, and they can frighten viewers.
5. **All brands are fictional** (GravityCo, FreshWay, Gravity Plus). Keep it that way.
6. **Length:** each voiceover is 240–265 words, which lands at 80–88 seconds at a brisk anchor pace (about 180 words a minute). If a cut runs long, trim the Mayor line first: the visual carries that joke.
7. **Hold each ticker item on screen about 2 seconds,** enough to tempt a pause.

### Posting playbook
- One episode a day, at the same time. Use a title card: **AUN • EP 01**.
- Put the series in a TikTok playlist called **AUN SEASON 1**.
- Pin a comment pointing at that episode's easter egg ("Did anyone read the ticker?").
- Reply to top comments **in character as Desmond**: *"We are aware of the hair. Do not adjust your reality."*
