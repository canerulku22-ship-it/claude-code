# Seasoned Chicken Spaghetti: full 3D animated cooking film

A 25-second, 1080 × 1920 (9:16), 30 fps fully CGI cooking commercial, built
entirely from Python inside Blender 5.2 (Cycles). There is no live footage in
it. Every ingredient, cooking action, noodle, particle, camera move, sound
effect and note of music is generated by the scripts in this folder.

It uses the same toolkit as `../chicken-burger-3d`. Compared with that film,
the 2D motion graphics are drawn at 70 % of the size (`UI_SCALE` in
`config.py`), the 3D end title is about 70 % as large, and the food is framed
smaller, so the frame breathes.

Every shot length is a multiple of 15 frames, so all cuts land on a beat of the
120 BPM soundtrack.

## The recipe

The film follows this recipe. Everything recipe-specific is in `config.py`:
the ingredient line-up and labels, the step titles, the end title and an
optional searing-temperature badge. That badge is off (`None`) by default;
set it only to a verified value.

1. **Season:** dice the chicken breast and season it.
2. **Sear:** sear the chicken in olive oil until golden.
3. **Sauce:** add garlic and onion, pour in crushed tomatoes, season with herbs and simmer.
4. **Boil:** cook the spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente.
5. **Toss:** toss the spaghetti in the sauce with the chicken.
6. **Serve:** plate it with basil and parmesan.

## Pipeline

```
build.py   -> out/chicken_spaghetti.blend (7 shot scenes + FULL_FILM edit scene)
           -> out/timeline.json           (overlay events, 3D anchor tracks, sound cues)
render.py  -> out/frames/<shot>/####.png  (Cycles, resumable)
audio.py   -> out/soundtrack.wav          (synthesised music + foley, cue-synced)
post.py    -> out/chicken_spaghetti_3d.mp4 (upscale, FX, motion graphics, H.264 + AAC)
```

`./make_all.sh` runs everything. Useful options:

```bash
python build.py --only S5_boil                        # iterate on one shot
python render.py --shots S5_boil --range 20-40        # partial / resumed render
python render.py --shots S7_final --scale 0.3 --samples 6 --step 10   # quick preview
python post.py --stills 120,450                       # composite single frames to PNG
```

### Requirements
* Python 3.13 with `bpy==5.2.3` (`pip install bpy==5.2.3`), or Blender 5.2 itself
* `numpy scipy pillow opencv-python-headless`
* `ffmpeg` (libx264 + aac)

Open `out/chicken_spaghetti.blend` to inspect any shot. Each shot is its own
scene, and `FULL_FILM` strings them together in the Video Sequencer. No baking
is needed: every particle system and every noodle is analytic, so any frame
renders on its own.

## How it is built

* **Spaghetti** (`cb/models.py`, `cb/fx.py`): each strand is an edge chain
  that Geometry Nodes turns into a round tube (Mesh to Curve, then Curve to
  Mesh) with the pasta material.
  * **Dry and boiling** (`FX_PastaDry`): every strand is an arc from its base
    point. `Fan` leans the bundle out against the rim. `Soft` increases the
    lean and curvature, so the strands slump, swirl and sink. A smooth radial
    clamp keeps them inside the pot.
  * **Tossed** (`FX_PastaPile`): random-walk noodles tangled into a mound
    fall in as a clump, settle, and jump in a staggered toss. Everything is
    computed from the frame number.
  * **Plated nest**: coils twirled around the axis and stacked into a mound.
* **Materials** (`cb/materials.py`):
  * Pasta has keyframed `cook` (dry and translucent, to soft and wet) and
    `sauce` (tomato spreading in organic patches with herb flecks) controls.
  * Chicken cubes have `season`, `sear` and `sauce` controls. Sear browns the
    cube edges first, using Cycles pointiness.
  * Chunky tomato sauce, garlic, onion, basil (translucent leaves with veins),
    parmesan with rind, ceramic, brushed steel, water and glass.
* **Liquids**:
  * The sauce surface rises in the skillet and its radius follows the flared
    pan wall.
  * The sauce pour is a bevelled curve with animated `bevel_factor_start` and
    `bevel_factor_end`.
  * Ring ripples and simmer bubbles are Geometry Nodes.
  * The water surface does not cast shadows, so the pot and pasta under it
    are lit without slow refractive caustics.
* **Particles** (`FX_Ballistic`, `FX_Loop`): spice, herbs, garlic, onion,
  salt, oil spatter, sauce and water droplets, boiling bubbles, steam and
  parmesan. Landings are ray-cast against the chicken cubes and the nest.
* **Post** (`post.py`):
  * Upscale, heat haze over the searing pan, bloom, grade, vignette, grain,
    and whip or zoom transitions on every cut.
  * All 2D graphics are drawn at native 1080 × 1920 and scaled by `UI_SCALE`.
* **Sound** (`audio.py`): fully synthesised.
  * Foley: searing sizzle, shaker rattles, sauce pour and simmering blups,
    a rolling boil, dry-pasta clatter, splashes, wet splats and whooshes.
  * Music: a 120 BPM groove with a build-up under the toss, ducked under the
    loudest cooking sounds.

## Render settings used for the delivered video

The delivered film was rendered at 60 % (648 × 1152) with 12 adaptive
samples and OIDN, on a 4-core CPU with no GPU, then upscaled to 1080 × 1920
in `post.py`, where all text is drawn at full resolution. On a machine with a
GPU, set `RENDER_SCALE = 1.0` and `SAMPLES = 64` in `config.py` for a
native-resolution master.
