# The Classic Spaghetti Bolognese: full 3D animated cooking film

A 23-second, 1080 × 1920 (9:16), 30 fps fully CGI cooking commercial, built
entirely from Python inside Blender 5.2 (Cycles). There is no live footage in
it. Every ingredient, cooking action, noodle, crumb of mince, camera move,
sound effect and note of music is generated by the scripts in this folder.

It is built with the same toolkit as `../chicken-spaghetti-3d`, with the same
smaller 2D graphics (`UI_SCALE = 0.7`) and food framed small in the frame.

## The recipe

1. **Soffritto:** soften finely diced onion, carrot, celery and garlic in olive oil.
2. **Brown:** add beef mince, break it up and brown it.
3. **Simmer:** deglaze with red wine, add crushed tomatoes and simmer.
4. **Boil:** cook the spaghetti in salted water until al dente.
5. **Toss:** toss the spaghetti in the ragù.
6. **Serve:** plate it with parmesan and basil.

The ingredient line-up, step titles and end title are all in `config.py`.

## Pipeline

```
build.py   -> out/spaghetti_bolognese.blend (7 shot scenes + FULL_FILM edit scene)
           -> out/timeline.json             (overlay events, 3D anchor tracks, sound cues)
render.py  -> out/frames/<shot>/####.png    (Cycles, resumable)
audio.py   -> out/soundtrack.wav            (synthesised music + foley, cue-synced)
post.py    -> out/spaghetti_bolognese_3d.mp4 (upscale, FX, motion graphics, H.264 + AAC)
```

`./make_all.sh` runs everything. See `../chicken-spaghetti-3d/README.md` for
the pasta, sauce and particle techniques, which are shared.

## What is new in this film

* **Soffritto** (`M.veg`, diced-vegetable prototypes):
  * Diced onion, carrot and celery are instanced particles, aimed so that
    each group lands in its own part of the pan.
  * Each vegetable has a keyframed `cook` control: raw, then translucent and
    golden with an oily sheen.
  * The whole layer is parented to a rotating empty, so it swirls with the
    wooden spoon.
* **Beef mince** (`M.mince`):
  * Thousands of irregular crumbles land in a spread across the pan.
  * Keyframed `brown` and `sauce` controls take them from raw pink-red with
    fat specks, to caramelised brown, to coated in ragù.
* **Per-shot prototypes** (`Shot.proto`): each shot gets its own copy of a
  particle prototype with its own animated material, so keyframed browning in
  one shot never leaks into another.
* **Deglazing**: a red-wine stream, a steam burst and a synthesised hiss.
* **Ragù** (`M.ragu`):
  * A deep red-brown sauce with meat and carrot texture.
  * The surface carries 1,300 sauced meat crumbles and rises with them.
* **Plated dish**: the nest is topped with a draped ragù cap studded with
  meat, plus basil and parmesan. The parmesan lands by ray-casting onto the
  combined envelope of nest and cap.
* **Sound**: new foley for the wine hiss and the wooden-spoon stirring.

## Render settings used for the delivered video

The film was rendered at 60 % (648 × 1152) with 12 adaptive samples and
OIDN, on a 4-core CPU, then upscaled to 1080 × 1920 in `post.py`, where all
text is drawn at full resolution. For a native-resolution master, set
`RENDER_SCALE = 1.0` and `SAMPLES = 64` in `config.py`.
